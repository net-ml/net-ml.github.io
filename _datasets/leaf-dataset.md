## Overview

Dataset accompanying the LEAF paper with over four years of cellular network KPI data from a major US metropolitan area.

## Content

- Multiple key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Urban and suburban environments
- Temporal data showing concept drift patterns
- Ground truth labels for drift detection evaluation
